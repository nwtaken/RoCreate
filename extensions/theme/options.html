RoCreate options

Backup and restore

Save your settings, watchlist and private game notes to a file, then restore them on another computer or after a reinstall. Backup files contain your notes: keep them private.

Cached data

RoCreate keeps stats, item prices and values on your device so pages load fast. Clearing it is safe: everything is fetched again when you need it.

Reset

Turn every setting back to its default and empty your watchlist. Private game notes stay on this device.
