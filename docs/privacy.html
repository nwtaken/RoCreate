Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 (profile social feeds added)

RoCreate is a browser extension that runs entirely on your own device. There is no RoCreate server and no RoCreate account. RoCreate processes the Roblox identifiers, page content, social links and optional virtual-currency information needed for its features. It has no developer-run backend, analytics or tracking, and it does not sell user data. Contextual lookups go directly to the services listed below.

What RoCreate reads

This can include Roblox account IDs, public usernames and profile content, the game or profile currently being viewed, and virtual Robux balances or transaction totals for tools you enable. RoCreate does not record a general browsing history, mouse movements or keystroke logs. Private game notes stay in local extension storage.

RoCreate only runs on roblox.com. It reads the pages you open there so it can restyle them and add its own sections.

To show your pending Robux, your groups' funds, inventories, mutual friends and the social links Roblox keeps on a person's communities and experiences, RoCreate calls Roblox's own APIs from the page. Those calls use the Roblox login you already have in your browser, exactly like the Roblox website does. RoCreate never reads, stores or sends your password or login cookie, and what Roblox returns stays in your browser.

The optional tools below also talk only to Roblox:

  • Quick jump sends what you type (after a short pause, at least two characters) to Roblox's own search for experiences, people, communities and Marketplace items, the same searches Roblox's search bar makes. Your last six picks stay on this device.
  • Money tools read the DevEx estimate from the transactions summary already on the page. CSV exports read your transactions, or a community's sales, audit log or member list when you are allowed to see them, from Roblox at about one request a second, and the file is built on your device.
  • Your experiences reads which communities Roblox lets you manage and your primary community, then the public experiences of those and your own, with their live player counts, from Roblox once a minute while a Home tab is open. The two-hour player trend is kept on this device.

What RoCreate stores

  • Private game notes are stored only in this browser, not in Chrome Sync. They are never included in requests to game-stat services. Optional backup files include them, so keep those files private.
  • The optional watchlist dashboard uses the same saved games, cached Creator Exchange figures and service permissions as the popup. CSV exports are created on your device.
  • Settings and your watchlist are kept in Chrome's extension storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome syncs them between your own devices.
  • Cached results (game stats, item prices, values, your Robux menu) are kept on your device so pages load faster. Each entry is refreshed or removed after a while.
  • Quick jump picks and the player trend for your experiences stay on your device; the trend keeps two hours of points.
  • Diagnostic notes about the last item scan and Robux menu load stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Access to roblox.com: to restyle Roblox and add RoCreate's sections.
  • Access to the services above: to load their numbers.
  • Storage: for settings and caches.
  • Alarms: a once-a-minute check that reloads the extension when its files were updated, and the spike alert check.
  • Changing one request header (declarativeNetRequest): YouTube's own API refuses requests that say they come from an extension, so RoCreate sets that one header on its own requests to youtube.com's API. It never touches your browsing.
  • Notifications: only for spike alerts and sales alerts, which are off until you switch them on in the popup. Spike alerts check your watchlist's player counts on Creator Exchange every 15 minutes. Sales alerts check your pending Robux and your groups' funds on Roblox every 15 minutes, with your own login, and the numbers stay on your device.

This website

rocreate.org has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. The download page builds your copy inside your browser from RoCreate's files on GitHub, and your choices aren't sent anywhere. The RoValra button links to its Chrome Web Store page. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
