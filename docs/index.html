Roblox, like the Creator Hub.

RoCreate is a free Chrome extension. It gives roblox.com a clean, flat look and adds the numbers creators care about: game revenue, your Robux, inventory value and more.

Download for Chrome View the code

Free and open source. Works alongside RoValra.

Features

Everything a creator checks, right where you look.

Game stats on every card

Every game card shows its estimated revenue with a trend arrow, plus total visits.

  • Point at a card's stats for exact numbers and a 7-day player graph.
  • Sort the Games page by revenue, visits, what's rising, players online, or revenue per player.

A stats strip on every game page

Estimated revenue, players and visits with this week's change, revenue per player, rating, global rank and average session time, plus a full 7-day player chart.

  • Copy the universe or place ID in one click.
  • Press Watch to follow a game from the toolbar popup.
  • Estimates come from Creator Exchange.

Profiles, rebuilt

Every profile gets a new layout, built from scratch:

  • Latest from them: their YouTube videos, Twitch streams, broadcasts and clips, and X posts, playable right on the profile. RoCreate finds the links in their bio, their experiences and the communities they own, and says where each came from.
  • A header with badges, live status, a "Live on Twitch" banner, counts, bio and links.
  • Their experiences with players and estimated revenue, communities, badges and favorites.
  • Their avatar and what they wear, inventory value with its full history, every official Roblox item they own with prices, and friends with mutuals first.
  • Native RoCreate sections throughout, with RoValra name colors, badges, pronouns, status, views, frames, avatar borders and private notes. Includes outfits, wearing categories, hidden experiences and community sorting.
  • Optional section shortcuts and search filters make long profiles easier to browse. Switch off the official item scan for lighter browsing.
  • Roblox's own layout is one click away, and its Add Friend and menu buttons keep working.

Community pages, summed up

Open any community and see its estimated revenue, players and visits across all its public games, plus a list of its top earners.

Everything in one popup

  • Compact settings groups remember what you opened. Switch features on or off, pick an accent color, or reduce RoCreate animations.
  • Streamer mode blurs your Robux and your account info until you point at them.
  • Your watchlist with live revenue and players, searchable and sortable. Open its optional dashboard for totals, sorting and CSV downloads.
  • Spike alerts: a notification when a watched game suddenly jumps.
  • What's new after every update. Open it with Alt+Shift+R.
  • An options page to back up and restore settings, watched games and private game notes.

A workspace for your watched games

Open the watchlist dashboard from the popup. Search by name or universe ID, compare player and estimated revenue totals, sort games and download a CSV.

Keep a private scratchpad for each game. Notes stay on this device, can be switched off, and are included in your own backup files.

Shown with example names and data.

Your Robux, in full

Open the Robux menu to see your pending Robux and the funds of every group you manage, with a total and roughly what it's worth through DevEx. Click a group to open its revenue page.

Switch on sales alerts and RoCreate tells you when your pending Robux or a group's funds go up.

Shown with example data.

Know who you're dealing with

  • Real Roblox staff get the Roblox logo next to their name, anywhere on the site.
  • Optional: accounts flagged on RobloxVerifieds, or that lost their verified badge, get a red badge with an X. Hover it for the reasons.

FAQ

Questions

Is it safe?

RoCreate runs only in your browser. There's no RoCreate server, no account and no tracking, and all of the code is open on GitHub. The privacy policy lists every site it talks to.

Does it work with RoValra?

Yes. RoCreate is built to run next to RoValra, and each keeps its own features.

Where do the numbers come from?

Game revenue estimates come from Creator Exchange, and inventory values from Rolimons. Everything else comes from Roblox itself. Revenue figures are estimates, not Roblox's official numbers.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. Until it is, you load it from the download page in three steps. The Chrome Web Store version will update itself.

How do I update?

Download again and unzip over the same folder. RoCreate notices the new version, reloads itself within a minute, and its popup shows what changed. Every release is on the changelog.

Does it slow Roblox down?

It only looks up what's on your screen, keeps the results on your device so pages load fast the next time, and waits out rate limits instead of hammering Roblox.

Why do some games have no stats?

Creator Exchange only tracks games with enough players. Smaller games simply show no stats.

Can I turn parts off?

Yes. Every feature has a switch in the popup. You can also leave features out entirely on the download page, and then RoCreate never contacts their services.

Is RoCreate made by Roblox?

No. RoCreate is an independent, free project and isn't affiliated with Roblox Corporation.
