FAQ

Questions

Is it safe?

It runs only in your browser. No RoCreate server, account or tracking, and all of the code is on GitHub. The privacy policy lists every site it talks to.

Does it work with RoValra?

Yes. RoCreate runs next to RoValra, and each keeps its own features.

Where do the numbers come from?

Revenue estimates come from Creator Exchange and collectible values from Rolimons. Everything else comes from Roblox itself.

Can I turn parts off?

Every feature has a switch in the popup. On the download page you can also leave features out entirely.

How do I update?

The Chrome Web Store version updates itself. If you installed a ZIP, download again and unzip over the same folder; RoCreate reloads itself within a minute. Every release is on the changelog.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

Yes. Add it from the Chrome Web Store and it updates itself.

Does it slow Roblox down?

It only looks up what's on your screen, caches results on your device and waits out rate limits instead of hammering Roblox.

Is RoCreate made by Roblox?

No. It's an independent, free project, not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.
