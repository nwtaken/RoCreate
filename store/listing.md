# Chrome Web Store listing draft

Name: RoCreate: Roblox, Rebuilt

Short description:
A cleaner Roblox with creator stats, rebuilt profiles, social feeds, watchlists and optional alerts. Works alongside RoValra.

Website: https://rocreate.org
Support: https://github.com/nwtaken/RoCreate/issues
Privacy policy: https://rocreate.org/privacy.html
Language: English
Price: Free

## Detailed description

Make Roblox feel like a creator workspace.

RoCreate gives roblox.com a clean dark interface inspired by Creator Hub, with optional tools for exploring games, following creators and keeping track of your own projects.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose an accent color, hide clutter, reduce RoCreate animations or enable streamer mode to blur your Robux balance and account information. Compact settings groups keep the popup easy to use.

EXPLORE GAMES WITH CONTEXT
See player counts, visits and estimated revenue from Creator Exchange. Compare games, sort supported game lists, view player-history charts and copy universe or place IDs. Community pages summarize their public experiences.

PROFILES, REBUILT
Browse experiences, communities, badges, friends, avatar outfits, collectibles value and official Roblox items in a cohesive layout. Search profile sections and jump between them with section shortcuts. Turn off the official-item scan for lighter browsing. Roblox's original layout remains available.

SOCIAL FEEDS ON PROFILES
When supported links are available, see YouTube videos, Twitch streams, broadcasts and clips on a profile. Play supported videos directly on the page. X posts appear only when its public feed responds. Links may come from a creator's bio, experiences or owned communities; RoCreate labels the source.

YOUR WATCHLIST, YOUR WORKSPACE
Watch games from their Roblox pages, then open a full dashboard to search, sort, compare totals and download a CSV. Keep private game notes on your device and include them in your own backup files.

WORKSPACE PAGES
Trades, transactions, settings, the Marketplace, communities and their admin pages, inventory, the avatar editor, messages, menus and dialogs share one flat Creator Hub style: an obsidian canvas, quiet panels, hairline borders and rounded corners. Profiles keep their own layout.

QUICK JUMP
Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) anywhere on Roblox to jump to any page or settings tab, your experiences and communities, or search experiences, people, communities and Marketplace items. It also runs quick commands, like turning streamer mode on.

YOUR EXPERIENCES
A Home table with the public experiences you own or manage: live players with a two-hour trend, visits, rating, favorites and links to the Creator Hub.

OPTIONAL ROBUX AND MONEY TOOLS
View pending Robux and managed community funds, see a DevEx estimate on My Transactions, and download CSV exports of your sales, purchases and payouts, or a community's sales, audit log and member list if you manage it. Files are built on your device. Player-spike and sales alerts are off until you turn them on.

Free and open source. No RoCreate account or subscription. Settings can use Chrome Sync; private game notes stay in this browser. Contextual lookups go directly to the services described in the privacy policy. There is no RoCreate backend or analytics.

RoValra is optional and installed separately. RoCreate reads supported RoValra profile customizations and presents them in its own layout. RoCreate is independent and is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or RoValra.

## Reviewer notes

The ZIP is the store build, not the unpacked development copy. It contains no development auto-reload loop, bundled RoValra, account credentials or remotely downloaded extension code.

Start on https://www.roblox.com/charts, then open an experience and a public profile. Theme and public information can be tested logged out. Friendship actions, mutual friends, personal Robux information and some official social links require the reviewer's own Roblox test account. No developer account credentials are provided or required.

Press Ctrl+K on any roblox.com page to open Quick jump; it lists pages without any request and searches Roblox only after typing. Logged in, Home shows the Your experiences table for accounts that own or manage public experiences, and My Transactions shows the DevEx estimate and CSV export card. Exports pace their requests and wait when Roblox rate limits.

Open the popup, expand Game tools, and open the watchlist dashboard. Add a watched game from its game page first. Private notes remain local. Expand Profiles and badges to control profile shortcuts, filters and the inventory scan. The scanner uses Roblox's existing limits and may show a wait state.

Third-party figures are estimates and may be unavailable or delayed. X refuses some public feed requests; the extension hides unavailable posts rather than attempting to bypass restrictions. Embedded players are YouTube/Twitch web pages inside roblox.com, not remotely loaded privileged extension code.

RobloxVerifieds features and its host permission are excluded from this first store build. The separate development/custom versions can include them. No Rotector or Rayward API integration is included.
