## RoValra (GPL-3.0)

RoCreate can load the [RoValra](https://github.com/NotValra/RoValra) browser extension by
NotValra. RoValra is **not bundled in this repository or in the installer**. When you choose
"Set up RoCreate with RoValra", the app downloads the current release from the Chrome Web
Store onto your own machine and loads it unpacked, so it stays under its own license
(GPL-3.0, with its image assets excluded) and is updated from the original source.
